When announcing repo-local features on the website, anchor constitution and principle-number references to the specific repository's constitution — the org constitution documented at /docs/getting-started/constitution/ has five principles, so unqualified citations of hero-constitution principles (e.g., "Principle VI, Deterministic Execution") don't resolve for site readers.
